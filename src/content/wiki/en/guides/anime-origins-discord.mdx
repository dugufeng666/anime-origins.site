## What this page answers

Players usually search Anime Origins Discord to find update posts, patch notes, or a community invite.

## The safest way to find it

1. Open the official Roblox page first.
2. Check the game description for current community links.
3. Confirm that the invite is still active before you save it.

## What to be careful about

- Random reposted invites often expire.
- Old community screenshots can point to dead links.
- Copying a Discord invite from a mirror site is risky unless you can confirm the source.
