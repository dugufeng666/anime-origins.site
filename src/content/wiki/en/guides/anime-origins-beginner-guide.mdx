## Quick answer

If you searched for an Anime Origins guide, use this order: claim the current codes, secure one dependable carry, add one support or economy unit, and save rare rerolls until your roster is clearer. The goal of the opening is a stable team, not a perfect collection.

## 30-minute opening plan

| Stage | What to do | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First login | Open the Roblox game from the official game page and confirm that the lobby and menus load normally. | It prevents spending time in the wrong result or an incomplete session. |
| Before spending | Claim the current [Anime Origins codes](/codes/anime-origins-codes), then note which rewards are gems, rerolls, or progression items. | Free resources change the value of your first decisions. |
| First team | Pick one carry and pair it with one support or economy-friendly unit. | Concentrated resources clear early content more reliably than a wide roster. |
| First checkpoint | Play the easiest available progression route and learn where units, traits, and upgrades are managed. | You need to understand the loop before chasing a meta build. |
| After the first run | Compare your units with the [Anime Origins tier list](/units/anime-origins-tier-list), then keep rare rerolls for the unit you expect to keep. | A tier list is more useful after you know what your roster is missing. |

## What to spend first

- Spend early resources on one reliable carry before filling every team slot.
- Add support or economy value when it helps the carry stay useful for longer runs.
- Use the [Anime Origins units](/units/anime-origins-units) page to separate a real upgrade from a duplicate role.
- Avoid chasing every new unit while your main carry is still underbuilt.
- Keep a reserve for the next progression checkpoint instead of treating the first shop visit as a reason to spend everything.

## What to unlock first

Prioritize the systems that improve your ability to play and evaluate the roster: the first progression route, unit management, traits, and the next available mode. Cosmetic or uncertain purchases can wait until you know which units and resources you actually use.

## Shop value

If you searched for `anime origins shop`, compare any purchase against the free rewards and the needs of your current roster first. A shop item is more useful when it improves the carry you already use or saves a meaningful progression step; it is less useful when it only adds another unit you cannot build yet.

This page does not claim permanent prices, drop rates, or best-value bundles because shop contents can change. Check the in-game description and current update context before treating a bundle as a good deal.

## Common mistakes

- Spending all rare currency before claiming codes or checking the current roster.
- Rerolling a temporary filler instead of saving for the carry you plan to keep.
- Building several damage units while leaving the team without support or economy value.
- Treating one tier-list row as a permanent rule after a patch or new unit release.
- Copying an old code from a search snippet without checking whether it still redeems.

## What matters most early

- One stable damage unit is better than three weak investments.
- Economy or support value matters because it buys you more room for mistakes.
- Traits and secret units matter later, not on the very first run.
- The tier list should guide your next upgrade, not your emotions.
