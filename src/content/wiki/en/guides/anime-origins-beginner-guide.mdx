## What this page is for

This page is the starting guide for the Anime Origins content cluster. It helps a new visitor understand the game fast enough to keep moving through the site without getting stuck on the wrong page.

## Early-game checklist

- Open the Roblox page and confirm you are in the right game.
- Claim the current codes before you spend anything.
- Keep one carry and one support instead of splitting resources across the whole roster.
- Learn which mode gives you the fastest early progress.
- Save rerolls and rare upgrade items until the roster is clearer.

## What matters most early

- One stable damage unit is better than three weak investments.
- Economy or support value matters because it buys you more room for mistakes.
- Traits and secret units matter later, not on the very first run.
- The tier list should guide your next upgrade, not your emotions.
