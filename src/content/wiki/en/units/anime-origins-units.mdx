## What this page is for

If you searched Anime Origins units, anime origins unit, or Roxi Anime Origins, this page is the roster map before you commit resources. It helps you identify what a unit does for a team, then sends ranking questions to the [Anime Origins Tier List](/units/anime-origins-tier-list/).

It is not a promise of permanent stats, drop rates, or patch-proof rankings. Use the current in-game unit card as the final check when a patch changes a name, ability, or banner.

## How to read the roster

| Role | What to look for |

## Roxi Anime Origins

Roxi is currently best treated as an early-fill or situational unit in the launch roster. If Roxi is one of your first usable pulls, keep the investment light: use Roxi to cover an open slot, but save scarce rerolls and major resources for a unit that clearly improves your main carry, support, or economy plan.

This is a role-fit call, not a claim about permanent rank. Check the current in-game card after a balance update, then compare Roxi against the rest of your roster using the [tier list](/units/anime-origins-tier-list/) and the six-role checklist below.

## Build a balanced unit team

A useful early Anime Origins team usually needs distinct jobs. Before replacing a unit, name the actual problem in the run.

| If your run fails because... | Check this job first | Do not solve it by... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waves stack too quickly | Main carry or coverage | Adding a second weak filler without changing the damage plan. |
| A strong unit still feels inconsistent | Support or utility | Rerolling every unit at once. |
| You cannot afford key upgrades | Economy | Replacing the only resource-helping slot with another damage name. |
| A newly pulled unit looks exciting | Open or flex slot | Selling your stable carry before testing the new role. |
