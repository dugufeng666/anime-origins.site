## What this page is for

This page is the broad units hub for Anime Origins. It is for players who want the roster map before they decide which unit page to open next.

## How to read the roster

| Role | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Carry | The unit that should do the most work in a team |
| Support | A unit that boosts the carry or keeps the run stable |
| Utility | A flexible slot that helps with speed, control, or survival |
| Limited | A banner or event unit that may not stay easy to obtain |

## Current roster map

The launch roster keeps clustering around a few names instead of one giant settled list. That is enough to build a useful hub page:

| Group | Names to watch |
| --- | --- |
| Headline picks | Cursed Lover, Valcrad, Madaro |
| Strong support / economy | Bon, Bluma, Goju, Shinoru, Mykie, Goki |
| Flexible core roster | Vegita, Aneko, Igritto, Sosuke, Tojei, Noroto, Starku, Yoto |
| Early fillers | Melio, Roxi |
