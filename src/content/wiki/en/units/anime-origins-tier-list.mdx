## What this page is for

This page should be the quick answer for players trying to decide who to build. If you searched anime origins wiki tier list, this is the page you want.

## Current launch snapshot

Anime Origins launched with a 37-unit roster, and public tier guides still keep circling the same core names. That is useful: it means searchers want a practical build answer, not a long lore page.

## Launch consensus

| Tier | Units | Why they land there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SS | Cursed Lover, Valcrad, Madaro | These are the launch names that keep showing up at the top of public tier lists. They are the safest long-term build targets if you want one unit to carry a lot of work. |
| S | Bon, Bluma, Tango | Strong utility and support names that help a roster move faster without forcing a narrow comp. |
| A | Vegita, Aneko, Igritto, Sosuke, Tojei, Noroto, Starku, Yoto, Goju, Shinoru, Mykie, Goki | Flexible roster pieces that become more valuable once you know your main carry and support slots. |
| B | Melio, Roxi | Early-fill or situational units. Fine for now, but not the names to chase first if you can get a higher-tier pull. |

## How to read the ranking

- If you want one safe build target, start with SS.
- If you want a balanced roster, S and A matter more than chasing every flashy name.
- If your roll lands in B, treat it as a temporary bridge rather than a final answer.
- Rank shifts after patches are normal; this page is a snapshot, not a promise.

## Trait patterns that keep showing up

Public launch guides keep linking the top units to a handful of traits:

- Immortal and Overseer for premium carries
- Rupture for crit-heavy damage
- Hustler for economy-linked value
- Stride for fast, cooldown-driven units like Tango
